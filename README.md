# 3D-Model-Marble-Run

A 3D-printable marble run I designed in Onshape for Hack Club's Half Life program. It was my second-week project.

An 8 mm marble starts at the top of a square spiral track, rolls down three straight runs connected by smooth 15 mm radius corners, and drops off at the end into a funnel. The funnel spins the marble down through the center hole into a catch cup on the base.

Built through 140 × 40 × 140 × 3 mm play for the base. The track is a C-shaped channel, 9 mm inner, 14 mm outer, open on top, swept along a path made of an angled sketch plane so each side slopes downhill.

The funnel was made through a revolved profile with a narrow neck, a flared wall, and a raised ring positioned under the end of the track of the catch cup (a ring on the base directly under the funnel's hole).
